📌 Executive Summary of Session Improvements

In this engineering session, we resolved core architectural limitations across the Dialogue Act Classification (DAC), Named Entity Recognition (NER), and AI Clinical Diagnosis Engine (OpenBioLLM-8B) without altering raw datasets or tag schemas.

1

Speaker-Aware De-Contamination Routing (Pipeline Engine)

Fixed cross-contamination where doctor screening questions (e.g., "any sugar, pressure, addictions?") were falsely logged as confirmed patient diseases, and resolved past conditions (e.g., "in 2018 rheumatoid arthritis") polluted the chief complaint. Added speaker role gatekeeping and prior consultation regex pattern matching.

2

Dynamic Dual-Architecture Adapter (DAC Inference)

Designed a backwards- and forwards-compatible adapter in dac_inference.py that automatically detects and loads both v1/v2 single-layer linear heads and v3 deep 2-layer MLP non-linear heads with dedicated [DOCTOR] and [PATIENT] speaker tokens (30,525 vocab size).

3

OpenBioLLM-8B Diagnostic Persona & Token Expansion

Resolved the safety refusal trap where Llama-3 stated "I cannot provide medical advice" by utilizing the official OpenBioLLM biomedical system prompt. Expanded token generation ceiling to 1024 tokens to eliminate truncation and added resilient regex parsers for natural clinical phrasing.

4

NER Training Loss Bug Diagnosis & v3/v2 Benchmarking

Identified the mathematical cause of the zero-F1 freeze during Kaggle training (token denominator dilution in padded sequences) and evaluated both NER v2 (0.7944 F1) and NER v3 (0.7650 F1) against baseline v1 (0.7544 F1).

  • Implemented BertDAC dual-head adapter supporting both nn.Linear (v1/v2) and 2-layer MLP nn.Sequential (v3).
  • Auto-detects classifier.0.weight in checkpoint state dictionaries.
  • Dynamically registers [DOCTOR] and [PATIENT] speaker tokens with vocabulary expansion to 30,525.
  • Added speaker turn formatting in predict().

2. pipeline/pipeline.py

  • Updated check_chief_complaint_condition to strictly filter out doctor queries (speaker == 'doctor').
  • Added prior consultation regex pattern matching ("in 2018", "after USG", "referred by doctor") to prevent past history from leaking into Chief Complaint.
  • Updated check_past_medical_history_condition to block doctor routine screening questions ("any diseases, sugar, pressure?").

3. pipeline/diagnosis_inference.py

  • Switched persona prompt to the official OpenBioLLM fine-tuned biomedical prompt, terminating safety refusals.
  • Increased num_predict from 256 to 1024 tokens.
  • Broadened regex parser to catch natural clinical narrative phrasing ("could be", "would include", numbered lists).
  • Fixed false-positive trigger on "blood test" / "blood pressure" matching gastrointestinal bleeding.

4. pipeline/sentence_processor.py

  • Preserved explicit speaker identity (Doctor vs Patient) on every parsed sentence object.
  • Ensured metadata passes through the full multi-stage aggregator.
