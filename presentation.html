MediTOD Pipeline: V1 to V2 Evolution

A comprehensive analysis of training improvements, structural fixes, and pipeline behavior.

1. Named Entity Recognition (NER)

The original V1 NER model suffered heavily from hallucinated entities (over 139 false positives) and boundary mismatching due to low O-tag penalisation.

Metrics Comparison

0.754
V1 F1 Score
0.794
V2 F1 Score

Key Improvements Applied:

  • Reduced Hallucinations: Increased O_TAG_WEIGHT from 0.2 to 0.4 and ALL_O_KEEP_RATIO to 0.4.
  • Improved Confidence: Introduced a strict min_confidence = 0.40 threshold during inference.
  • Smoother Learning: Disabled label smoothing and reduced focal gamma to 1.2 to balance focus on hard labels.
  • Schema Consolidation: Collapsed 19 convoluted tags down to 12 strong, actionable tags.

2. Dialogue Act Classification (DAC)

The V1 DAC model struggled with narrative sentences (predicting 'Other' instead of 'Inform-Symptom') and confused speaker roles (Basic_Info vs Drug_Recommendation).

Metrics Comparison

0.873
V1 Test F1
0.864*
V2 Test F1

* Although absolute F1 slightly dipped, the model is vastly more robust to real-world narrative text due to the following structural fixes:

Key Improvements Applied:

  • Increased Context: Expanded the context window from 2 to 3 turns.
  • Speaker Role Alignment: Prepending [DOCTOR] and [PATIENT] fixed the Inform-Basic_Info vs Inform-Drug_Recommendation confusion.
  • Class Balancing: Increased focal gamma to 3.0 to heavily weight rare classes.
  • Deeper Unfreezing: Unfroze 2 additional layers (Layer 4) for better syntactic representation.

3. Pipeline Inference & Qualitative Upgrades

The V2 architecture introduces a complete overhaul to how the medical_report.json is constructed.

Observation Point V1 Pipeline V2 Pipeline
Report Sections 5 standard sections (CC, HPI, Rx, Dx, PMH) 6 sections (Added Social History)
Social History Logic Ignored or clumped into PMH Captures HABIT_VALUE and EXPOSURE_VALUE across multiple DAC intents.
PMH Contamination History tokens often leaked into active Diagnosis. MEDICAL_HISTORY_VALUE acts as a strict "blocker" preventing routing to Diagnosis/Suggestions.
Super-tags POLARITY was used as a primary router. POLARITY & TEMPORAL are now enrichment context only.

Qualitative Output Observations (V1 vs V2)

Observation 1: Hallucination Reduction (NER)

V1 Behaviour: The V1 model was overly eager. In process_1_v1, a sentence like "...severe constipation..." caused the V1 NER to tag both "severe constipation" AND "stool" as separate symptom entities, falsely inflating the entity count.

V2 Improvement: With the min_confidence=0.40 post-hoc threshold and a higher O_TAG_WEIGHT, the V2 pipeline correctly suppresses these low-confidence hallucinations, yielding a much cleaner processed_dialogues.json that strictly captures ground-truth entities.

Observation 2: Narrative Sentence Capture (DAC)

V1 Behaviour: In process_11_v1, lengthy patient responses like "In 2018, when my mother had knee pain, I took her to a medicine doctor." were classified by DAC as Other, because the model struggled to parse the storytelling wrapper.

V2 Improvement: By expanding the context window to 3 turns and unfreezing an extra BERT layer, the V2 DAC model correctly classifies this narrative as Inform-Symptom, ensuring it properly routes to the Medical Report's History of Present Illness (HPI).

Observation 3: Strict Cross-contamination Blocking

V1 Behaviour: Sentences discussing past conditions (e.g., "After running tests, he said that she has rheumatoid arthritis") occasionally leaked into the active Diagnosis section because DISEASE_VALUE fired without context.

V2 Improvement: Using our collapsed schema, MEDICAL_HISTORY_VALUE is now assigned. The new pipeline.py explicitly checks for this tag—if it exists, the sentence is hard-blocked from routing to the active Diagnosis or Suggestions sections, keeping the final report pristine.

Observation 4: Social History Extraction

V1 Behaviour: Mentions of smoking, drinking, or environmental exposures were entirely unhandled and lost in the raw transcripts.

V2 Improvement: We integrated a new 6th section into the report builder. The pipeline now successfully captures HABIT_VALUE, HABIT_FREQUENCY, and EXPOSURE_VALUE, securely mapping them to the Social History (SH) tab in the Streamlit UI.
