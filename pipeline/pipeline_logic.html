POLARITY and TEMPORAL are cross-cutting super-tags: they appear alongside entities in every report section and do not independently route a sentence. They are used as enrichment, never as primary routing signals.

Report Section Routing Logic

1 Chief Complaint (CC) AND logic

Captures what brought the patient in. Requires both a symptom-class NER entity and a symptom-informing DAC intent.

NER (any of):
SYMPTOM_VALUE   SYMPTOM_LOCATION
DAC (any of):
Inform-Symptom   Inform-Basic_Information

Why only these NER tags? In the collapsed schema, DISEASE_VALUE has been merged into SYMPTOM_VALUE, so conditions like "diabetes" and "hypertension" are automatically captured here. SYMPTOM_LOCATION provides anatomical grounding ("chest pain", "lower back").

POLARITY co-occurrence: If POLARITY appears alongside SYMPTOM_VALUE (e.g., "no fever"), the sentence still routes to CC but should be rendered with the negation preserved in the report ("Patient denies fever").

2 History of Present Illness (HPI) AND logic

Captures the timeline, quality, and pattern of the current complaint. The NER tags here are modifiers of symptoms, not the symptoms themselves.

NER (any of):
SYMPTOM_CHARACTERISTICS   SYMPTOM_FREQUENCY   SYMPTOM_LOCATION   TEMPORAL
DAC (any of):
Inform-Basic_Information   Inform-Symptom   Inform-Etiology

TEMPORAL routing: In the old schema, SYMPTOM_WHEN was HPI-exclusive. Now that it is collapsed into TEMPORAL, the DAC intent disambiguates: TEMPORAL + Inform-Symptom → HPI, while TEMPORAL + Inform-Existing_Examination_and_Treatment → PMH. The tag alone is never sufficient.

Overlap with CC: SYMPTOM_LOCATION legitimately appears in both CC and HPI. A sentence like "burning pain in the chest since two weeks" satisfies both sections — the pipeline should route it to both CC (for the symptom) and HPI (for the characteristics + temporal detail).

3 Past Medical History (PMH) Hierarchical OR

Uses a three-path hierarchical acceptance strategy. A sentence qualifies via any path.

Path A — Strong NER Signal (Free NER):
Contains MEDICAL_HISTORY_VALUE OR MEDICAL_TEST_NAME → Accept regardless of DAC tag.
Rationale: These tags have near-zero false positive rates (precision >90% in our model). If the NER model says "tuberculosis 5 years ago" is MEDICAL_HISTORY_VALUE, trust it.
Path B — Strong DAC Signal (Free DAC):
DAC = Inform-Existing_Examination_and_Treatment → Accept if any non-O NER entity exists.
Rationale: This intent almost always indicates discussion of prior tests, surgeries, or treatments. Even if NER only picks up TEMPORAL or MEDICATION, the sentence belongs in PMH.
Path C — Conditional DAC (requires NER confirmation):
DAC = Inform-Basic_Information OR Inform-Medical_Advice OR Inform-Drug_Recommendation OR Inform-Precautions
→ ONLY accept if MEDICAL_HISTORY_VALUE is present.
Rationale: These DAC tags are broad — they could describe current treatment or past events. We only route to PMH when NER explicitly confirms historical content.

TEMPORAL enrichment: If TEMPORAL co-occurs with MEDICAL_HISTORY_VALUE (e.g., "had TB five years ago"), include the temporal span in the PMH report entry.

4 Social History (SH) NER-driven

Captures lifestyle, habits, and environmental exposures. NER is the primary routing signal; DAC acts as a soft filter.

NER (any of):
HABIT_VALUE   HABIT_FREQUENCY   EXPOSURE_VALUE
DAC (any of — soft filter):
Inform-Basic_Information   Inform-Symptom   Inform-Etiology   Inform-Existing_Examination_and_Treatment
Exclusion:
If MEDICAL_HISTORY_VALUE is also present → route to PMH instead (medical history takes precedence over social context).

POLARITY + HABIT_VALUE: Sentences like "no, I don't smoke" contain POLARITY + HABIT_VALUE. The negation should be rendered in the report ("Patient denies smoking"). This combination is clinically important — absence of risk factors is as valuable as their presence.

Wide DAC acceptance: Social history questions from doctors frequently get classified as Request-Etiology or Inform-Etiology ("What kind of work do you do?" → understanding occupational exposure). The DAC filter is intentionally broad here.

5 Diagnosis (Dx) Strict AND + Blocker

The strictest routing. Requires a doctor-side diagnosis intent and a disease/symptom entity, with explicit blockers to prevent past-history contamination.

DAC (required — one of):
Diagnose   Inform-Medical_Advice
NER (required — any of):
SYMPTOM_VALUE
Blocker (must NOT contain):
MEDICAL_HISTORY_VALUE

Why SYMPTOM_VALUE for diagnosis? In the collapsed schema, DISEASE_VALUE was merged into SYMPTOM_VALUE. So "diabetes", "hypertension", "GERD" are all SYMPTOM_VALUE entities. The Diagnose DAC intent is what distinguishes a diagnosis statement from a symptom report.

Speaker awareness: Per our DAC error analysis, Diagnose is almost always a doctor utterance (100% accuracy in test set). If speaker information is available, use it as additional validation — patient utterances should never route to Dx.

6 Medications & Suggestions (Rx) AND + Blocker

Captures prescriptions, precautions, and follow-up advice from the doctor. Requires both a recommendation-class DAC intent and medication or advice NER entities.

DAC (any of):
Inform-Drug_Recommendation   Inform-Precautions   Inform-Medical_Advice
NER (any of):
MEDICATION   MEDICAL_TEST_NAME
Blocker:
MEDICAL_HISTORY_VALUE — If present, sentence describes past medication, not a current prescription. Route to PMH instead.

Why include MEDICAL_TEST_NAME? Doctors frequently recommend tests alongside medications: "Get an HbA1c done and continue metformin." When the DAC is Inform-Medical_Advice, a test name is a recommendation, not history.

DAC-only fallback: If DAC is Inform-Precautions but NER finds no MEDICATION or MEDICAL_TEST_NAME (e.g., "avoid spicy food", "take rest"), still accept the sentence — precautions are often NER-light lifestyle advice. Use a soft rule: Inform-Precautions alone → accept with lower confidence.

Design Principles (v2)

  • POLARITY and TEMPORAL never route alone. These super-tags absorbed 5+ legacy status/when tags. They enrich the sentence once it is routed by a primary entity, but are never the sole routing signal.
  • MEDICAL_HISTORY_VALUE is a hard blocker for Dx and Rx sections. This prevents past conditions from contaminating current diagnoses ("had TB five years ago" ≠ current diagnosis) and past medications from appearing as current prescriptions.
  • DAC-first for Dx. Diagnosis requires Diagnose intent — this is the highest-precision DAC class (100% accuracy). The NER alone cannot distinguish "patient reports diabetes" from "doctor diagnoses diabetes."
  • NER-first for SH. Social history tags (HABIT_VALUE, EXPOSURE_VALUE) are semantically unique — they never appear in other clinical contexts. NER is the primary signal; DAC is a soft filter.
  • Dual-routing is allowed. A sentence can appear in both CC and HPI (e.g., "severe burning chest pain since two weeks"). This avoids information loss.
  • Inform-Precautions is DAC-sufficient for Rx. Lifestyle advice ("avoid spicy food") may not contain any entity but is clinically important. Accept with lower confidence.
